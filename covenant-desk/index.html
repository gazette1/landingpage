Covenant Desk: a quarterly loan review where every covenant figure traces to the loan agreement

A post-close surveillance workflow on one synthetic retail loan. It recomputes the borrower's compliance certificate under the loan agreement's own definitions, reconciles the quarter's package against the lender's prior review and the servicing record, defers the covenant result when an open document conflict would change it, and renders the review in two lender templates. Click any figure to see its formula and the line it came from.

Synthetic data. Prototype built by Russell Harris. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CarbonAI. Not a CarbonAI product and does not describe how CarbonAI works.
