Trace Desk: an acquisition screen where every figure traces to its page

A screening workflow on one synthetic broker package: an OM, a messy rent roll, a T-12, two retail leases and estoppels, and a lender term sheet. It bridges the broker's NOI to an underwritten NOI under a written firm policy, shows where the numbers and the paper disagree, tests whether an open disagreement changes the screening decision, and renders the IC screen memo in two firm templates. Click any figure to see the formula and the document line it came from.

Synthetic data. Prototype built by Russell Harris. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Harvey. Not a Harvey product.
