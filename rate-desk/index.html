Rate Desk: were supplier bills above the signed contract rate?

A Diagnostic pack for one synthetic distributor: the question, the business definitions, the metrics and their bases, acceptance criteria, and a rollout plan, with every figure computed by a contract-to-invoice check that you can rerun under a changed definition. 2Desk: the review queue

One row per finding (supplier and explanation type). Expected amounts come only from signed terms; the PO is shown beside the contract result but never governs. The note under each finding was drafted by an AI model from that finding's facts and checked by a gate; edit it and the gate runs again. The model cannot change an outcome or an amount.

Decisions

Every proposed recovery needs Approve, Reject, or Defer, every review item needs a disposition note, and every note must pass the gate before approved claims go to the AP manager. Nothing is paid, short-paid, or sent to a supplier from this page. Reviewer decisions are logged but never used as labels by the evals: approvals are not ground truth, because finance users are often lenient.
